A safer daily routine

Care that starts with good information.

The right product is the one that suits the person's needs, instructions, and advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

  • Keep a current list of medicines and supplements.
  • Read the active ingredient, directions, and warnings on every label.
  • Ask a pharmacist or clinician before combining medicines or starting a supplement.
  • Store medicines securely and follow the package storage instructions.
Medication safety: Older adults may be more sensitive to side effects and interactions. This store does not provide a diagnosis or replace advice from a pharmacist or clinician. Never change a prescribed medicine without consulting the prescriber.
Helpful answers

Shopping and safety FAQs

Can I use an over-the-counter product with a prescription?

Check with a pharmacist or clinician first. OTC medicines and supplements can interact with prescription medicines and with each other.

Are these products a substitute for medical care?

No. Product information is for general shopping support only. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical questions.

How should I choose a mobility aid?

Consider the person's balance, strength, home layout, and ability to use the aid safely. A physical therapist or clinician can help assess fit.

How do I check a medicine's directions?

Read the full package label, including active ingredients, dosage, warnings, and expiry date. If anything is unclear, ask a pharmacist.
